# A room with a view: Automatic assessment of window views for high-rise high-density areas using City Information Models and deep transfer learning

## Abstract

However, existing methods are labor-intensive, inaccurate, and non-scalable to assess 6 window views in high-rise, high-density cities. This study aims to assess Window View 7 Indices (WVIs) quantitatively and automatically by using a photo-realistic City Information 8 Model (CIM). First, we define four WVIs to represent the outside (i) greenery, (ii) water-9 body, (iii) sky, and (iv) construction views quantitatively. Then, we proposed a deep transfer 10 learning method to estimate the WVIs for the window views captured in the CIM. 11 Preliminary experimental tests in Wan Chai District, Hong Kong confirmed that our method 12 was highly satisfactory (R² > 0.95) and fast (3.08 s per view), and the WVIs were accurate 13 (RMSE < 0.042). The proposed approach can be used in computing city-scale window views 14 for landscape management, sustainable urban planning and design, and real estate valuation. 15
