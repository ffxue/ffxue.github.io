2.2 Semantic Metrics (Domain-Free)
 Similarity
More closely connected  more similar

 Metrics
Path: Inverse of (path length + 1)
 sim(hill, coast) = 1/(1+4) = 0.20

Resnik (1995):

 sim = ½ × Information of common

 sim(hill, coast) = -ln(0.00176)=6.34**

Lin (1998):



 sim = Info of common / Info of description
 sim(hill, coast) =

2×ln(0.00176)/(ln(0.000189)+ln(0.0000216)) = 0.59
