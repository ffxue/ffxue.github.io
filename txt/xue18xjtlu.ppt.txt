2.2 A derivative-free optimization (DFO) approach
 Semantic registration is a decision task
 Can be automated through optimization

 Problem formulation (Xue et al., 2018a)
 Input 1: E.g., 3D point cloud
 Variables (X): transformation parameters

DFO-based Semantic
registration (Xue et al.,
2018a; 2018b)

 Objective function (f): minimum geometric error
 Constraints (C): Topological regularity

 DFO algorithms (Conn et al., 2009)
 Solves problems comprising too complex derivatives
 Succeeded in many science and engineering problems
o E.g., Protein folding (Nicosia & Stracquadanio, 2008),
aircraft wing design (Lee, et al., 2008)
