1.2 Urban semantics
 Why semantics from signals? (Rowley & Hartley, 2017)
 Answering interrogative questions (what, who, where, when)
 Enabling automated reasoning / checking
 Abstracted, processed from data and signals

Data: Digital pixels
(0~255 R, G, B)

 Types of urban semantics
 Geometric: Dimension, location, rotation, color, …
 Non-geometric facts: Function, materials, history, owner, …
 Instructions (how-to): Manufacturing, installation, access, …

 Common databases / interfaces
 BIM/CIM: building/city information model
 GIS: geographic information system

1.3 Roofs
 Considerable in typical metropolises
 20~25% urban surfaces (Rose et al. 2003)

 Rooftop albedo
 Reflected (other than absorbed) solar radiation by coatings
o  Negative radiative forcing

“Cool” roofs (Source: Lawrence Berkeley
Laboratory)

 At a worldwide level, every year (Akbari et al. 2009)
o Offsets billions of tons of CO₂ emissions
o billions of dollars of energy bills

 Became mandated in certain areas

o E.g., California Energy Commission (2005)

 Problems in CIM roofs

 No albedo, even not accurate in 3D
