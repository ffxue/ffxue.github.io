3.1 A recap
 Urban point cloud
 Has advantages for buildings/urban applications
 Semantics is a must-to-do for DT

 Semantics: Class labels, symmetry, objects, relationship
 Can be detected in urban point clouds
o With / without training data sets
o With / without existing 3D resources

 Powerful for understanding a point cloud if detected
o Sometimes better than the factual (e.g., cars, chairs)

 Auto DTing of buildings/city in early stage
 Wide frontier of urban semantics to explore toward 100% autonomy
 More values lie in simulation/optimization
Xue: DT. Leica Geosystems Hong Kong 2022, 25 Nov. 2022.
