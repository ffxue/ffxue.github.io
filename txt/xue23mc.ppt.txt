2 Opportunity
 Minecraft Education Edition (MCEE)
 Programmable education sandbox platform for 3D worlds
o A world’s top popular game (180M monthly active players)
o Multi-user interaction (LAN/Internet server)
o Familiar by Gen-Z

o With Python/Scratch coding interfaces

 Free for all HKU staff/student accounts

 Available for laptop (Mac, Win), iPad, phones

6 Summary
